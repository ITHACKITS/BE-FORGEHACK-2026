### 1. D24: Mastin et al. 2009 mass eruption rate ($M = c \cdot H^p$)
- **SPEC.md says:** $c = 140$, $p = 4.14$, with status `to_verify`.
- **From the primary paper** (Mastin et al. 2009, JVGR 186, doi:10.1016/j.jvolgeores.2009.01.008):
  1. The original equation is $H = 2.00 \cdot \dot{V}^{0.241}$, where $\dot{V}$ is the dense-rock-equivalent (DRE) magma volume rate in $\text{m}^3/\text{s}$.
  2. DRE magma density $\rho = 2500\ \text{kg/m}^3$.
  3. Inverting for the mass rate: $\dot{M} = \rho \dot{V} = 2500 \times (H / 2.00)^{1 / 0.241} = 2500 \times 2^{-4.14938} \times H^{4.14938}$.
  4. The exact exponent is $p = 1 / 0.241 = 4.14938 \approx 4.15$ (not 4.14).
  5. The exact coefficient is $c = 2500 \times 2^{-4.14938} = 140.85\ \text{kg/s}$ (or $140.78$ with $p = 4.15$).
  6. **Reference for $H$:** Mastin et al. state that $H$ is measured **above the vent**, not above sea level. For Anak Krakatau (vent at ≈110 m above sea level), $H = H_{asl} - 0.11\ \text{km}$. A satellite plume top of $15.2\ \text{km}$ asl gives $H = 15.09\ \text{km}$.
  7. The uncertainty of about a factor of 4 (50% interval) is confirmed in the paper. D24 moves from `to_verify` to `sourced`.

### Source D24 (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jvolgeores.2009.01.008)
- **Status:** `V`
- **Verified at:** `2026-10-05T13:21:54.214222+00:00`
- **Notes:**
  - Primary paper opened and verified: Mastin et al. 2009, JVGR 186(1–2), pp. 10–21.
  - **Original equation:** H = 2.00 × V_dot^0.241 (equation 1 in the paper).
  - **Definitions:**
    - H is the eruption column height **above the vent** (km above vent level), not above sea level.
    - V_dot is the volumetric output rate as dense-rock-equivalent (DRE) tephra (m³/s).
  - **DRE density:** ρ = 2500 kg/m³ (used explicitly by Mastin et al. for DRE magma).
  - **Inversion to mass rate (MER):** V_dot = (H / 2.00)^(1/0.241) = (H / 2.00)^4.1493776.
  - **Exponent p:** p = 1 / 0.241 = 4.14938… (rounded 4.15, not 4.14).
  - **Coefficient c:** M_dot = ρ × V_dot = 2500 × (1/2.00)^4.1493776 × H^4.14938 = 2500 × 0.056338 × H^4.14938 = 140.85 kg/s. With p = 4.15, c = 2500 × (1/2)^4.15 = 140.78 kg/s (rounded 141 or 140.8 kg/s).
  - **Uncertainty:** Mastin et al. report an empirical scatter of about a factor of 4 (50% interval).
  - **Critical finding:** c = 140 and p = 4.14 in SPEC.md are rough approximations of 140.85 and 4.15 (1/0.241). Also, for Anak Krakatau (vent ≈110 m above sea level), H_vent = H_asl − 0.11 km.
